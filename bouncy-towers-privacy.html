Bouncy Towers · iOS

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 15, 2026

Bouncy Towers is a free iPhone game made by Benjamin Kushner. This page explains what information the game uses, who handles it, and the choices you have. The game has no accounts and never asks for your email, contacts, photos or exact location.

Saved on your device

The game saves your coins, high scores (Classic and Arcade), unlocked balls and backgrounds, and settings like sound, game mode and colorblind mode. This stays on your iPhone and is deleted if you delete the game.

Leaderboards (LootLocker)

The online leaderboards run on LootLocker. When you open the game, it signs you in as a guest, which creates an anonymous player ID. Your best scores are sent to LootLocker with that ID. If you choose a player name, it is shown publicly on the leaderboards next to your scores, so please don't use your real name or anything personal.

Ads (Unity Ads)

The game shows ads from Unity Ads. To show and measure ads, Unity may collect:

  • Device identifiers, such as your advertising identifier if you allow tracking, or an identifier for the app on your device
  • Approximate location (country level), based on your IP address
  • Device details, like model, iOS version, language, screen size and connection type
  • How you interact with ads, and in-app purchase history
  • Performance data about the ad software

Unity's handling of this information is described in the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. You can limit ad tracking on your iPhone in Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Tracking.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. The game only receives confirmation of what you bought, so it can unlock it and restore it later.

Children

Bouncy Towers is made for a general audience. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has entered personal information as a player name, contact us and we will remove it.

Removing your leaderboard data

To have your player name and scores removed from the leaderboards, email us with your player name. Deleting the game removes everything saved on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
