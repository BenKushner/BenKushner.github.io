About the Game

Bouncy Towers is a color-matching arcade game built for iOS. You slide your finger to move a ball across the screen, timing your bounces to land on towers that match your ball's color. Each successful match scores a point, but as your score climbs, the speed increases and colors change faster — keeping the pressure on.

The game features a global leaderboard so players can compete for the top spot, along with a character and background store to personalize the experience. It also has an in-app purchase for unlocking all character skins. I designed, developed, and published this game entirely solo.

What I Learned

Bouncy Towers taught me a lot about the full pipeline of shipping to the App Store — from building for iOS in Unity, to navigating App Store Connect, to handling the review process. I also got hands-on experience with monetization (in-app purchases), online leaderboard integration, and designing touch controls that feel responsive and intuitive on a small screen.
