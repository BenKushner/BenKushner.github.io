Privacy Policy

Clutch · Effective November 21, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Clutch app for mobile devices ("the Application"), published by Jamin Games LLC ("the Service Provider"). It explains what the Application stores, what its third-party services collect, and the choices you have.

The Short Version

  • Clutch has no accounts. It does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your career, settings, Buckets and Locker items are saved on your device only.
  • Ads are optional rewarded videos. They play only when you choose to watch one.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. The Service Provider never sees your payment details.

Information Stored on Your Device

The Application saves your game data on your device: your player and career, game settings, your Buckets balance, items you own in the Locker, and tutorial progress. This data is not sent to the Service Provider. If you delete the Application, this data is deleted with it and cannot be recovered.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Application offers optional rewarded video ads, for example to double your rewards after a game or to claim free daily Buckets. These ads are served by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), IP address, general location derived from IP address, device and operating system details, and ad interaction and diagnostic data.

On iOS, the Application asks for your permission before any tracking takes place. If you decline, ads may still be shown but are not personalized using your advertising identifier. You can change this choice at any time in your device's Settings under Privacy & Security, Tracking.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. The Application uses RevenueCat to confirm what you have bought so that purchases such as Clutch Pro can be restored. RevenueCat receives purchase records and an anonymous app user identifier. It does not receive your name or payment details.

RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy

What the Application Does Not Do

  • It does not gather precise information about the location of your device.
  • It does not use Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies to process your data.
  • It does not sell your personal information.
  • It does not include social features, chat or user-generated content shared with other players.

Third Party Access

The third-party services named above handle data under their own privacy policies. The Service Provider may also disclose information if required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena or similar legal process, or when it believes in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect its rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, or investigate fraud.

Your Choices

  • You can play without ever watching an ad or making a purchase.
  • You can decline tracking when asked, or turn it off later in your device's Settings.
  • You can stop all collection of information by uninstalling the Application.

Data Retention

Game data stays on your device until you delete the Application. The Service Provider does not hold a copy of it. Data collected by Google AdMob and RevenueCat is retained according to their policies. If you have a question about data connected to your use of the Application, contact support@jamingamestudio.com and the Service Provider will respond in a reasonable time.

Children

The Application is not directed at children under the age of 13, and the Service Provider does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. Ads are limited to general-audience content. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the Application, please contact support@jamingamestudio.com so that the necessary steps can be taken.

Security

The Service Provider is concerned about safeguarding the confidentiality of your information. Because the Application keeps game data on your device and has no accounts, there is no Service Provider server holding your personal information.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Continued use of the Application after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about privacy while using the Application, please contact the Service Provider by email at support@jamingamestudio.com.
