About Me

A bit about
who I am

Hey! I'm a graduating senior in RPI's Games and Simulation Arts and Sciences program with a passion for making games people actually want to play. I love the intersection of creative storytelling and technical problem-solving.

I've shipped a multiplayer game on Steam with 5,000+ downloads, published 4 games on the iOS App Store, and have been building games since 2020. I'm always looking for the next challenge.
