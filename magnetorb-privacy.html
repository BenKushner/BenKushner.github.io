MagnetOrb · iOS

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 21, 2026

MagnetOrb is a free iPhone game published by Jamin Games LLC. This page explains what information the game uses, who handles it, and the choices you have. The game never asks for your email, contacts, photos or exact location.

Saved on your device

The game saves your coins, blue gems, unlocked orb skins, achievements, stats, best scores and settings. This stays on your iPhone and is deleted if you delete the game.

Leaderboards (Unity Gaming Services)

The hourly, daily and all-time leaderboards run on Unity Gaming Services. When you open the game, it signs you in anonymously, which creates a player ID. Your scores are sent to Unity with that ID. The player name you choose is shown publicly on the leaderboards next to your scores, so please don't use your real name or anything personal.

Ads (Unity Ads)

The game shows ads from Unity Ads. To show and measure ads, Unity may collect:

  • Device identifiers, such as your advertising identifier if you allow tracking, or an identifier for the app on your device
  • Approximate location (country level), based on your IP address
  • Device details, like model, iOS version, language, screen size and connection type
  • How you interact with ads
  • Performance data about the ad software

You can limit ad tracking on your iPhone in Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Tracking.

Other information Unity may receive

Because the game connects to Unity's services, Unity may also receive technical and usage information, such as your IP address, app version, when and how long you play, and crash reports. Unity's handling of all of this is described in the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Purchases

MagnetOrb has no in-app purchases. Coins and blue gems are earned by playing, and the game never handles payment details.

Children

MagnetOrb is made for a general audience. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has entered personal information as a player name, contact us and we will remove it.

Removing your leaderboard data

To have your player name and scores removed from the leaderboards, email us with your player name. Deleting the game removes everything saved on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
