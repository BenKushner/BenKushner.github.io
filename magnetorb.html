About the Game

MagnetOrb is a hyper casual game built around a simple but satisfying core mechanic — an orb bouncing between two magnets that you toggle on and off by tapping the screen. The orb travels back and forth, and your job is to collect coins while avoiding obstacles scattered across the field.

Collecting coins in quick succession builds a combo multiplier, but if you go too long without grabbing one, the combo resets. Hitting the walls also keeps your combo alive, adding a layer of strategy to how you navigate. As your score climbs, things speed up and obstacles get denser, keeping the pressure on.
